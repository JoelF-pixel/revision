## What you need to know

- Magnetic Fields
- The magnetic field is the area surrounding a magnet where the force
- is acting on another magnet or magnetic material. It can be observed
- using a compass placed at different points around a bar magnet.
- The field lines can be drawn by using the compass to mark the direction at
- a range of points.
- A magnet always causes a magnetic material to be attracted. The strength
- of the magnetic field is determined by the proximity to the magnet.
