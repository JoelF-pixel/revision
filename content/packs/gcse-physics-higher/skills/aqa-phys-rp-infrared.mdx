## What you do (method)

- Compare emission/absorption using different surface finishes (matt black vs shiny).
- Measure temperature change with a thermometer/temperature probe.
- Keep distance/time/starting temperature controlled.

## Common mistakes

- Not controlling variables between trials.
- Confusing *emitters* and *absorbers* (good emitters are good absorbers).
