## What you need to know

- Conservation of Energy
- Energy can never be created or destroyed, just transferred from one form
- to another. Some energy is transferred usefully and some energy gets
- transferred into the environment. This is mostly wasted energy.
- Power
- Power is the rate of transfer of energy – the amount of work done in a
- given time.
- power = energy transferred ÷ time
