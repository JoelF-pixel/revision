## What you do (method)

- Measure mass of the block/water.
- Supply a known power for a measured time (or measure energy directly if given).
- Measure temperature change.
- Calculate energy transferred: **E = P × t**.
- Calculate SHC: **c = E / (m × ΔT)**.

## Common mistakes

- Mixing units (g vs kg, °C vs K for changes).
- Using the starting temperature instead of **ΔT**.
- Forgetting heat losses: results are often *lower* than expected.
