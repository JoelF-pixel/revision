## What you need to know

- pressure of the gas.
- If the temperature of the gas increases, then the pressure will also
- increase. The hotter the temperature, the more kinetic energy the gas
- particles have. They move faster, colliding with the sides of the container
- more often.
- Density
- Density is a measure of how much mass there is in a given space.
- Density (kg/m³) = mass (kg) ÷ volume (m³)
