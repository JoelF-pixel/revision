## What you do (method)

- Build a circuit with variable resistor, ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel.
- Vary the current and record paired readings of **I** and **V**.
- Plot **I–V** graph and describe shape for:
  - resistor (straight line through origin)
  - filament lamp (curve; resistance increases with temperature)
  - diode (one-direction; threshold)

## Common mistakes

- Swapping ammeter/voltmeter positions.
- Not zeroing meters / reading wrong scale.
- Plotting axes the wrong way (be consistent; state what you plotted).
