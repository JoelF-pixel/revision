## What you do (method)

- Use light gates or video to measure speed at different times/positions.
- Calculate acceleration: **a = Δv / Δt**.
- Plot v–t graph and use gradient for acceleration.

## Common mistakes

- Using total time instead of the interval for Δv.
- Not using consistent units (m/s, s).
