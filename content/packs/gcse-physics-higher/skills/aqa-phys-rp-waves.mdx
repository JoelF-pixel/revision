## What you do (method)

- Measure wavelength (e.g. ripple tank or slinky).
- Measure frequency (count waves per second or use generator setting).
- Calculate speed: **v = f × λ**.

## Common mistakes

- Measuring λ between the wrong points.
- Mixing Hz and kHz; m and cm.
