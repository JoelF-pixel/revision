## What you do (method)

- Measure mass using a balance.
- Measure volume:
  - regular solid: use ruler → calculate volume
  - irregular solid: displacement can / measuring cylinder
- Calculate density: **ρ = m / V**.

## Common mistakes

- Mixing units (cm³ vs m³; g vs kg).
- Reading meniscus wrong in measuring cylinder.
