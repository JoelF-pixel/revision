## What you do (method)

- Measure the spring’s natural length.
- Add known weights (force = weight) and measure extension.
- Plot force vs extension and identify the limit of proportionality.

## Common mistakes

- Measuring total length instead of **extension**.
- Not spotting the non-linear region beyond proportionality.
