## What you need to know

- Systems
- Energy can be transferred in the following ways:
- mechanically – when work is done;
- electrically – when moving charge does work;
- heating – when energy is transferred from a hotter object to a colder
- object.
- Conservation of Energy
- Energy can never be created or destroyed, just transferred from one form
