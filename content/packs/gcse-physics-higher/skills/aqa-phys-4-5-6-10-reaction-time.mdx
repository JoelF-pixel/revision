**Spec reference:** 4.5.6 (subtopic).

## What you need to know

- Reaction time is the time taken for the driver to
- respond to a hazard. It varies from 0.2s to 0.9s
- between most people.
- Reaction time is affected by:
- • tiredness
- • drugs
- • alcohol
- • distractions
