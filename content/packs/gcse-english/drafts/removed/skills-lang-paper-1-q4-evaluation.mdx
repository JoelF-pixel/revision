## Key steps (method)

- Decide your viewpoint: **agree/disagree to what extent** and why.
- Choose 3–4 points that support your judgement (each with strong evidence).
- For each point:
  - quote
  - AO2 zoom-in
  - explain how it supports your evaluation
- Include one counterpoint if it strengthens your judgement (Tier 3 move).
- Keep returning to the statement in the question (don’t drift).

## Common mistakes

- Summarising instead of evaluating.
- Saying “I agree” but not analysing the evidence.
- Being extreme with no nuance (“completely agree”) when the text is mixed.
- Writing lots but not staying tied to the statement.
