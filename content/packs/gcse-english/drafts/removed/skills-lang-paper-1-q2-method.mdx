## Key steps (method)

- Identify the focus (the specific part of the extract + what it’s describing).
- Pick **2–3 short quotes** that contain strong word choices.
- For each quote: zoom in on 1–2 key words and explain **what it suggests**.
- Name a method only when useful (metaphor, personification, contrast, etc.).
- Link each paragraph back to the focus (what we learn/feel/see about the thing being described).

## Common mistakes

- Retelling what happens (AO1) instead of analysing words.
- Using long quotes with no close analysis.
- Saying “it makes the reader want to read on” (usually weak).
- Not answering the specific focus of the question.
