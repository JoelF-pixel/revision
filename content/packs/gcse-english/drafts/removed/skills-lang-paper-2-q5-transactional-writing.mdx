## Key steps (method)

- Identify the **format** (letter/speech/article) + **audience** + **purpose**.
- Choose your viewpoint and write a one-sentence **position**.
- Plan 3–4 paragraphs (each with a clear argument point).
- Use **rhetorical devices** with intent: rhetoric questions, repetition, emotive language, statistics, anecdotes.
- Vary sentence types for control (short punchy lines + longer developed ones).
- Leave 2 minutes to check SPaG and paragraphing.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting the audience (tone too casual/too formal).
- Listing devices without a clear argument.
- Writing one giant paragraph.
- Not using enough persuasive/engaging language.
