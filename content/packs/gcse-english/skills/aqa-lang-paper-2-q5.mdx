## What you need to know

- Q5 is **transactional writing**: you’re writing to argue/persuade/advise.
- You’re marked on:
  - **Content + organisation** (A05)
  - **Technical accuracy** (A06)

Method (fast + exam-safe):
1) Decide your **viewpoint** and audience.
2) Plan 4 chunks: **Intro → 2–3 main points → counterpoint → punchy ending**.
3) For each paragraph, plan 1–2 rhetorical methods:
   - rhetorical question, direct address, rule of three, contrast, anecdote, emotive language, facts/statistics.
4) Leave 3–5 minutes to proofread (A06).

## Common mistakes

- No clear viewpoint (waffle / balanced forever).
- Writing like a story instead of persuasive writing.
- Overusing rhetorical questions with no substance.
- Not proofreading: basic errors that cost easy A06 marks.

## Self-check

- My viewpoint is clear in the first 2–3 lines.
- Paragraphs are logically ordered and linked.
- I’ve used a range of persuasive techniques *purposefully*.
- I proofread for: sentence boundaries, commas, spellings, paragraphing.
