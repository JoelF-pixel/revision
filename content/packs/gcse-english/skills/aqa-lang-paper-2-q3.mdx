## What you need to know

- Q3 compares **how** writers use language in both sources.
- Best structure: **point-by-point comparison** (not “A then B”).
- Method:
  1) Pick 2–3 focus areas (tone, attitudes, imagery, word choices).
  2) For each, select a short quote from A and a short quote from B.
  3) Analyse a word/phrase from each, then compare the effect.

Sentence frame:
- “In Source A, the writer uses ___ (‘__’) which suggests ___. In Source B, ___ (‘__’) suggests ___. This creates a more ___ / less ___ tone…”

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting to compare (writing two mini-analyses).
- Naming techniques without effect.
- Picking quotes that don’t match the question focus.

## Self-check

- I compared in every paragraph.
- I zoomed into at least one key word/phrase from each source.
- I explained the effect on the reader.
- I linked back to the question focus each time.
