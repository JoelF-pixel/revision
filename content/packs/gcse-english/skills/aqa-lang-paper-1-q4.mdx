## What you need to know

- Q4 = **evaluation**. You take a view and prove it with evidence.
- Use a clear stance: **I agree / disagree / partly**.
- Use **methods + effects** (language/structure) to support your judgement.

## Common mistakes

- Sitting on the fence without a judgement.
- Retelling the story.
- Only analysing language (structure can be powerful here too).

## Self-check

- Did I make my viewpoint clear in the first lines?
- Did I use **multiple references** and explain why they support my view?
