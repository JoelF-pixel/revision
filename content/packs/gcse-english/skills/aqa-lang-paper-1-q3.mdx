## What you need to know

- Analyse **structure**: how the text is put together (beginning → middle → end).
- Track **focus shifts**: setting, character, perspective, pace.
- Use structural methods: **zoom in/out**, contrast, cyclical structure, sentence length, paragraphing.

## Common mistakes

- Doing language analysis instead.
- Listing techniques without linking to overall effect.

## Self-check

- Have I covered **start/middle/end**?
- Have I explained how structural choices affect the reader?
