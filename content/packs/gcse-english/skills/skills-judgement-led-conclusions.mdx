## Key steps (method)

- Restate your judgement in a **sharper** way (not the same sentence).
- Zoom out to writer’s overall message/purpose.
- Mention the most powerful idea your essay proved (one sentence).
- Optionally add one final nuance: *Ultimately…, yet…* (only if it clarifies).

## Common mistakes

- Re-listing paragraphs (“Firstly… secondly…”).
- Adding brand-new quotes/analysis.
- Ending with a generic moral (“This teaches us to be kind”).
