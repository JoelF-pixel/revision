## What you need to know

- Q4 asks you to compare **viewpoints/perspectives**.
- It’s usually worth a lot of marks, so do **3–4 strong comparative paragraphs**.
- Method:
  1) Identify each writer’s viewpoint in 1 sentence.
  2) Choose 3 comparison angles (attitude, tone, ideas/values).
  3) For each angle: quote A + quote B, analyse language/structure, then compare.

Good connectives:
- *Whereas, however, in contrast, similarly, this suggests, this implies*

## Common mistakes

- Just summarising what happens.
- Comparing topics rather than viewpoints.
- Using evidence from one source only.
- Not analysing methods (AO2-style analysis is what lifts the marks).

## Self-check

- I stated each writer’s viewpoint clearly.
- Each paragraph uses **both sources**.
- I analysed language (and at least one structural choice) and linked it to viewpoint.
- I end by comparing the overall message/attitude.
