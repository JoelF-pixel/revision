## Key steps (method)

- Decide your target mark and write a time plan before you start.
- Spend a fixed amount of time on:
  - reading the question + highlighting + micro-plan
  - writing (main marks)
  - checking/editing (especially for SPaG)
- If you’re stuck, write a simpler paragraph with a clear quote + AO2 zoom rather than freezing.

## Common mistakes

- Spending too long reading/annotating and running out of writing time.
- Writing one huge paragraph and leaving others half-finished.
- Not leaving time to check SPaG (easy marks left on the table).
- Over-planning (a plan should be quick and usable).
