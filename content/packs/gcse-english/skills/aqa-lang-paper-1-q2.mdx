## What you need to know

- Analyse **language**: zoom in on **words/phrases**, methods, and effects.
- Use short quotes, then explain: **what it suggests** + **why it’s effective**.
- Prioritise: **connotations**, semantic fields, tone, imagery.

## Common mistakes

- Feature spotting without meaning.
- Quoting huge chunks.
- Saying “it makes the reader want to read on” (too generic).

## Self-check

- Have I used **2–3 strong quotes** and explored them properly?
- Have I linked effect back to the question focus?
