## What you need to know (Love & Relationships poetry)

- Build comparison using a clear thesis + integrated paragraphs.
- Memorise **short micro-quotes** and attach 2–3 AO2 comments to each.
- Use AO3 lightly and only when it sharpens a method point.

## Common mistakes

- Writing two separate mini-essays (one poem then the other) instead of **comparing throughout**.
- Over-quoting: long quotes instead of micro-quotes + analysis.
- Only spotting techniques but not explaining the **meaning/effect** (AO2).
- Forcing context even when it doesn’t help (AO3).
- Not planning: you end up repeating the same point.

## Self-check

- I have a thesis that compares both poems on the theme.
- Each paragraph uses **both poems** (point + quote from each).
- I zoom in on words/structure and explain how meaning is created.
- I comment on structure (shifts, endings, tone change) at least once.
- I finish by comparing what each poet suggests overall.
