## Key steps (method)

- End each paragraph with a **1-sentence link** back to the exact wording of the question.
- Use variety:
  - *This reinforces the idea that…*
  - *Therefore, Shakespeare/Priestley suggests…*
  - *This supports the view that…*
- Link to your thesis/judgement (not just the paragraph point).

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting to link back at all (paragraphs feel disconnected).
- Copying the question word-for-word every time.
- Linking back vaguely (“This shows the theme”).
