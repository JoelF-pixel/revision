## What you need to know

- Q2 is a **summary of differences or similarities**.
- You must use **both sources** (and usually a specified section of each).
- Simple method (safe + high scoring):
  1) Re-read the question and underline the focus (e.g. attitudes, experiences, feelings).
  2) Draw a 2-column table: Source A / Source B.
  3) Find **3 clear points** (each point must be supported by text evidence).
  4) Write 3 comparative sentences/mini-paragraphs: *Both… / However A… whereas B…*

## Common mistakes

- Writing about only one source.
- Copying huge quotes instead of summarising.
- Making inferences that aren’t supported by the text.
- Listing points without comparing.

## Self-check

- I used **both sources** in every point.
- Each point is a summary, not analysis of language.
- I used comparative connectives: *both, similarly, whereas, however*.
- My points directly answer the question focus.
