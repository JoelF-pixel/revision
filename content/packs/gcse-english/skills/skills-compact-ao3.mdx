## Key steps (method)

- Use AO3 as a *because*:
  - *This reflects… because…*
  - *For a (Jacobean/Victorian/Edwardian/post-war) audience, this would…*
- Keep it **one sentence**.
- Attach it directly to the quote’s meaning (not general background).

## Common mistakes

- Writing a mini history lesson.
- Dropping AO3 at the end with no link.
- Getting context muddled (wrong period/purpose).
