## What you need to know

- Choose a clear **concept** (mood/setting/conflict) and stick to it.
- Control structure: opening hook → build → turning point → ending.
- Use **varied sentence forms** and deliberate punctuation for effect.
- Show don’t tell: concrete nouns, sensory detail, strong verbs.

## Common mistakes

- Overcomplicated plot.
- Random techniques without control.
- Forgetting paragraphs / ending abruptly.

## Self-check

- Do I have a clear voice and consistent tense?
- Have I varied sentences and used paragraphing intentionally?

## Practice (teacher routine)

1) Plan under time (5 minutes)
2) Expand the plan into a **methods plan** (structure + language + punctuation + sentence structures)
3) Write a **small chunk** (opening / descriptive paragraph / key moment)
4) Write the full response under time (45 mins)
5) A06 drill: practise correct punctuation + sentence variety
6) Pre-learn reusable paragraphs (weather / water / tree / character) and slot them in when useful
