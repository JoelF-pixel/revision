## What you need to know (A Christmas Carol)

- Quote revision is essential (AO1): revise in **pairs/groups** so you can reference flexibly.
- For each quote: plan **3 critical comments on effects** (AO2).
- Authorial intent (AO3) should be woven through your argument: message → method → audience impact.

## Common mistakes

- Turning it into a plot summary (AO1).
- Only describing Dickens’ ideas without analysing **methods** (AO2).
- Dropping context facts (workhouses/poverty) without linking them to Dickens’ purpose (AO3).
- Using vague language: “makes the reader think…” without *what* they think and *why*.
- Ignoring the question focus (e.g., writing about Scrooge when the question is on the Cratchits).

## Self-check

- I have a clear argument about Dickens’ **message**.
- I’ve selected quotes that are short enough to analyse properly.
- Each paragraph includes at least one **method** (contrast, symbolism, narrative voice, structure).
- I link AO3 to Dickens’ intent (what he’s criticising / persuading the reader to do).
- My conclusion links back to the question and Dickens’ overall purpose.
