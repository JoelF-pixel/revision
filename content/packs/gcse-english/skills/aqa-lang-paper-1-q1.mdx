## What you need to know

- Q1 is **retrieval**: 4 things from a specific part of the source.
- Use **short, precise** points.
- Only use what’s **explicitly** in the text.

## Common mistakes

- Inference instead of retrieval.
- Using info from the wrong lines.
- Writing long explanations (not needed).

## Self-check

- Can I point to the exact line for each of my 4 points?
- Have I given 4 distinct things?
