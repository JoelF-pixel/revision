## Key steps (method)

- **P (Point):** answer the question in one sentence.
- **E (Evidence):** embed a short quote.
- **E (Explain/Effect):** zoom in on 1–2 words + explain what they suggest.
- **Z (Zoom / link):** link to theme/purpose and back to the question.

## Common mistakes

- Writing a point that doesn’t answer the question.
- Dropping a quote with no analysis.
- Explaining the plot instead of writer’s choices.
- Forgetting to link back to the question.
