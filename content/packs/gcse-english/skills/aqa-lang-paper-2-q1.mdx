## What you need to know

- This is the **easy marks** question. Don’t overthink it.
- Read the question carefully: it tells you which **source** to use and what to look for.
- Use a quick method:
  1) Read the 4 statements.
  2) Scan the correct lines in the source and **underline evidence**.
  3) Decide True/False based on *exact wording*.
  4) If stuck: choose the option that is **directly stated**, not implied.

## Common mistakes

- Using the wrong source / wrong part of the text.
- Choosing based on what “sounds right” instead of what’s stated.
- Missing negatives/quantifiers: *never, only, all, some, mostly*.

## Self-check

- I used the right source.
- I can point to the exact line that proves each answer.
- I re-checked any statement with a negative (not/never) or absolute word (all/only).
