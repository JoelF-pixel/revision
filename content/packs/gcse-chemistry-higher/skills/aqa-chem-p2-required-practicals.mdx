## Key steps (method)

- Know the **aim**, **apparatus**, and **variables** (IV/DV/control) for each required practical.
- Be ready to answer **evaluation** questions: uncertainty, errors, improvements.
- Write methods like a mark scheme: clear steps, repeats, and what to record.

## Common mistakes

- Writing vague evaluation (“human error”) rather than specific measurement limits.
- Missing a key control variable.
- Not linking improvements to what they improve (accuracy / precision / validity).

## Exam-style questions (quick)

1) Identify IV/DV/control variables for a given practical.
2) Suggest two improvements and explain how they reduce uncertainty.
3) Describe what results you’d expect if a variable is increased.
