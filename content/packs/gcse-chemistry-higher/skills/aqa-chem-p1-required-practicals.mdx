## Key steps (method)

- Learn the **aim**, key **apparatus**, and key **variables** (IV/DV/control).
- Know the **risk assessment** basics (corrosive acids, flames, hot apparatus).
- Write a clear **method** and include repeats / averages where relevant.
- Practicals are often assessed via **analysis + evaluation** (errors, improvements).

## Common mistakes

- Not controlling variables (e.g. concentration, temperature, surface area).
- Vague evaluation (“human error”) instead of specific sources of uncertainty.
- Forgetting quality of measurements (resolution, repeats, anomalies).

## Exam-style questions (quick)

1) Identify IV/DV/control variables for a given practical and explain why.
2) Describe a method to make a pure dry salt and how to check it’s pure.
3) Suggest improvements to reduce uncertainty in a rate practical.
