## Key steps (method)

Use collision theory to explain rate changes:

- **Temperature** ↑ → particles move faster → more collisions + more have Ea.
- **Concentration/pressure** ↑ → more particles per volume → more collisions.
- **Surface area** ↑ → more exposed particles → more collisions.
- **Catalyst** → lower activation energy → more successful collisions.

## Common mistakes

- Saying catalysts make collisions “more energetic” (they change Ea).
- Forgetting pressure only affects gases.
- Not linking surface area to collision frequency.

## Exam-style questions (quick)

1) Explain why increasing temperature increases rate (collision theory).
2) Explain why powdered marble reacts faster than chips.
3) Explain the effect of concentration on reaction time.
