## Key steps (method)

- Decide if the process is **exothermic** or **endothermic**.
- For required practical / calorimetry: calculate energy using **q = m c ΔT**.
- Link energy profile diagrams to activation energy and overall energy change.

## Common mistakes

- Using the wrong sign for ΔT or mixing endo/exo descriptions.
- Forgetting to convert units (e.g. mass in g vs kg) or using the wrong c.
- Confusing activation energy with energy released.

## Exam-style questions (quick)

1) Use q = m c ΔT to calculate energy change for a temperature rise.
2) Sketch an energy profile for an exothermic reaction and label Ea.
3) Explain how catalysts affect activation energy and rate.
